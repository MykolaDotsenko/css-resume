How I work

Engineering decisions under uncertainty

Evidence before fixes

Reproduce, trace the authoritative data, measure scope, then change the smallest necessary boundary.

Correctness over guesses

Keep ambiguity explicit and prefer review, fallback, or unknown states to plausible-but-wrong automation.

Recoverable systems

Favor idempotent writes, retry-safe flows, validated boundaries, and visible degraded behavior.

Proportional architecture

Add frameworks, services, state layers, or AI only when the product earns the complexity.

Product lens

I tend to build around the decision point: messy information → structured state → explicit uncertainty → clear next action.

Connect

Backend, data integrations, or operational software?
