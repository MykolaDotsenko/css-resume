Working style

How I tend to work

Check the data first

If production data looks wrong, I try to reproduce it and trace the source before touching the implementation.

Do not guess on identity

If two records cannot be matched safely, I would rather send the case to review than make a confident-looking bad merge.

Make syncs safe to rerun

Imports and sync jobs should be able to run again after a partial failure without creating a second problem.

Use the simple option when it is enough

If Django or plain HTML/CSS solves the problem, I do not add another service or framework just for the sake of it.

I enjoy

Work where the data is messy but the person using the product still needs a clear answer about what changed and what they should do next.

Connect

I’m interested in backend, data, and operational software.

Especially when several systems have to agree on the same business data.
